How the projection works

Year one income is your investment times the yield. From year two, the dividend per share grows by the rate you enter. Reinvested dividends buy more shares at a flat $1 price, so your income compounds; with reinvesting off, dividends are kept as cash instead. Ending value is your shares (at $1) plus any cash kept.

The projection excludes taxes, trading fees, share-price changes, dividend cuts and special payouts. A dividend yield and growth rate you type in are assumptions, not forecasts — companies can cut or stop paying at any time. This is maths, not advice.
