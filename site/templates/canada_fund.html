Canada Strong Fund Watch: can I buy it yet?

Last updated: {{ updated_h }}. We update this page when Ottawa publishes something new. General info, not advice.

Short answer: no, not yet

Status: not open. There's no launch date. As of Oct 21, 2026, the federal government hadn't said when the Canada Strong Fund will launch or exactly how it will work (CBC News).

Next date to watch: Nov 5, 2026. That's when the Finance Minister tables Budget 2026 (Department of Finance Canada). The minister's spokesperson told CBC the fall budget will include a "significant" update on the fund.

What's been announced

  • Prime Minister Mark Carney announced the fund in April 2026 with seed funding of $25 billion over three years.
  • It's meant to invest only in Canada, be arm's-length from government, and fund major projects in areas like energy, infrastructure, agriculture and technology.
  • It's expected to include a "retail investment product" so ordinary Canadians can buy in. The details haven't been announced.
  • Who will run it (the CEO and board) hasn't been announced. The government still has to create a Crown corporation for it.

Source: CBC News, Oct 21, 2026.

The government told CBC the retail part is taking time because there's no existing template for it from other countries.

⚠️ Scam warning

There is no way to buy into the Canada Strong Fund today. If anyone offers you "early access", a "pre-sale" or guaranteed returns from it, by ad, text, email, phone or social media, treat it as a scam. Don't send money or personal details.

When it does open, the details will come from the Government of Canada (canada.ca). Report suspected fraud to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.
