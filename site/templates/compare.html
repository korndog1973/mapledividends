How the projection works

Year one starts with the latest regular dividend repeated at its usual frequency. Dividend growth applies from year two. Prices move at the rate you enter. The model uses evenly spaced payments, fractional shares, and a fixed exchange rate. For USD payouts, that rate converts every dividend into CAD.

Ending value includes any dividends kept as cash. Cumulative dividends show all payouts earned, including reinvested ones. Scenarios exclude special payouts, taxes, trading fees, and currency changes. Companies need a known payout currency and frequency to appear in the calculator.
