How it works

Every time the site rebuilds (twice a day), we look at each company's regular dividend per share and compare it with the one before, the same way our Deadly / Not Deadly list does. The biggest percentage cut filed in the last {{ weeks }} weeks goes on the sap bucket. The biggest raise goes on the pancake flag. If there's been no cut (or raise) in that window, we show the most recent one with its date.

Each change is dated by the company filing that first announced the new rate. Changes we can't date, because a declaration in between is missing from our data, are left out. Special dividends don't count.

The movers under the art are TradingView's free widget: delayed share-price moves for the day, not dividends. It loads from TradingView when you scroll to it.

For fun, not advice. A dividend cut isn't a reason to sell and a raise isn't a reason to buy. Check the company's own announcement first.
