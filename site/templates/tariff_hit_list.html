How we build this list

  • Every row comes from the company's own press release, quarterly report or investor presentation, or, where noted, its results call. The date shown is the publication date.
  • We quote the figure the company gave. We don't estimate, adjust or add up anything.
  • Softwood lumber figures include long-running U.S. anti-dumping and countervailing duties as well as tariffs, because that's how the companies report them.
